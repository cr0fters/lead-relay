# Privacy Policy

Effective date: February 12, 2026

LeadRelay ("we", "us", "our") provides lead capture and customer messaging tools for businesses. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect personal information when you use our website, widget, and related services.

## 1. Information we collect

We may collect:
- Contact information: name, email address, phone number.
- Message content: chat messages and conversation history submitted through the widget or WhatsApp.
- Project and lead details: project summary, timeline, budget, location, and other business inquiry details.
- Account information: owner login email and authentication-related records.
- Technical data: IP address, browser/device metadata, timestamps, and basic usage logs.

## 2. How we collect information

We collect information when:
- You submit information through a LeadRelay-powered widget.
- You communicate through WhatsApp channels connected to LeadRelay.
- Site owners or operators view, edit, or respond to leads in the owner portal.
- You interact with our website or services.

## 3. How we use information

We use information to:
- Capture and manage customer leads.
- Route inquiries to the correct business owner or tenant account.
- Enable two-way messaging and follow-up communications.
- Operate, monitor, secure, and improve our services.
- Provide account security features such as login and password reset.
- Comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms.

## 4. Legal bases (where applicable)

Depending on your jurisdiction, we process personal information based on:
- Consent.
- Performance of a contract.
- Legitimate interests (for service operation, fraud prevention, and improvement).
- Compliance with legal obligations.

## 5. AI-assisted processing

We may use AI providers to help summarize or structure inquiry data and support conversational workflows. Personal information included in messages may be processed by those providers solely to deliver the service functionality.

## 6. How we share information

We may share information with:
- Service providers (hosting, infrastructure, email, analytics, and messaging platforms such as WhatsApp/Meta).
- Integrated AI providers used for conversation and summarization features.
- Business customers (tenants/site owners) receiving their own lead data.
- Legal authorities where required by law.

We do not sell personal information for money.

## 7. Data retention

We retain personal information only as long as needed to provide services, fulfill legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements. Retention periods may vary by data type and customer configuration.

## 8. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to protect personal information. However, no internet transmission or storage system is completely secure.

## 9. International data transfers

Your information may be processed in countries other than your own. Where required, we use appropriate safeguards for cross-border data transfers.

## 10. Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to:
- Access personal information.
- Correct inaccurate information.
- Delete personal information.
- Restrict or object to processing.
- Request portability.
- Withdraw consent (where processing is based on consent).

To exercise rights, contact us using the details below.

## 11. Children’s privacy

Our services are not directed to children under 13 (or higher age where required by local law), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## 12. Third-party services

Our services may integrate with third-party platforms (including WhatsApp/Meta). Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies.

## 13. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the updated version and revise the effective date.
